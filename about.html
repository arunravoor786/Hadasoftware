About Hadasoft

A software company building platforms that businesses depend on.

Hadasoft engineers production-grade software for two of the hardest problems any business faces: keeping customers, and seeing risk before it lands. We pair deep technical capability with the operational discipline to run real products at scale.

Who we are

Built to deliver, not to demo.

Hadasoft was founded on a simple conviction: most business software over-promises and under-delivers. Companies are sold dashboards they never use and automations that quietly break. We build the opposite — systems that earn their place by doing real work, every day, reliably.

Our two products reflect that. BizWhats keeps customers coming back for small and medium businesses. ACIP catches risk for construction enterprises before it becomes loss. Different industries, same standard: software that works in the real world, under real constraints.

How we work

The principles behind every build.

🎯

Solve a real problem

We only automate work that someone is already paying to do manually. If no one does it today, it's a feature, not a product.

⚙️

Storage-mediated systems

Every component communicates through a database — testable, replayable, observable. This is how software survives past launch.

✅

Prove every layer

We smoke-test and commit a working state before building the next layer on top. Slow at first, then suddenly fast.

✂️

De-scope without apology

A focused product that works beats a sketch of everything. We cut to ship the parts that earn trust first.

🌐

Multilingual at the schema

We build for India's languages from the database up — not as an afterthought translation layer.

🛡️

Security is non-negotiable

Credential rotation, audit trails, and safety guardrails are built in from the first commit, not added later.
