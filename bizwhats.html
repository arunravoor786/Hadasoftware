BizWhats — the platform that keeps your customers coming back.

A WhatsApp-first customer retention platform. BizWhats finds the right customers, reaches them in their own language, converts them, and keeps them engaged — automatically. Built for Indian SMBs, and configurable for any business that depends on repeat customers.

What BizWhats does

Retention is the whole product.

Acquiring a customer is expensive. Keeping one is where the profit is. BizWhats runs the entire retention loop so business owners don't have to.

🔍

Find

Discovers and scores high-intent leads from public sources, ready for outreach.

💬

Reach

Sends personalised WhatsApp messages in the customer's native language.

🤝

Convert

Manages the reply thread and moves prospects from trial to paid via Razorpay.

🔁

Retain

Keeps customers engaged with timely, automated re-engagement and check-ins.

Built once, fits everywhere

A retention engine that adapts to any business.

BizWhats was built first for Indian small and medium businesses — salons, gyms, coaching classes, clinics, boutiques, kirana stores. But the underlying engine is sector-agnostic. Any business that depends on customers returning can run on BizWhats.

Salons & Spas Gyms & Fitness Coaching Classes Clinics & Healthcare Boutiques & Retail Kirana Stores Restaurants Service businesses
How it works

Six engines. One automated pipeline.

Every engine writes to the database; the next reads from it. No fragile chains — each stage is independently testable, replayable, and observable.

1

Scout

Pulls leads from Google Maps, JustDial, IndiaMART, and SerpAPI. Normalises numbers to E.164 and tags each lead's language.

● In production
2

Enrich

Scores each lead on completeness and intent signals, surfacing the hottest prospects first.

● In production
3

Outreach

Sends first-touch WhatsApp messages in the lead's native language, with sector-tailored demos.

● Active
4

Follow-up

Keeps threads alive, classifies reply intent, and escalates hot conversations to a human inbox.

○ Rolling out
5

Close

Handles the trial-to-paid moment through Razorpay and activates the customer account.

○ Rolling out
6

Retain

Runs periodic check-ins, monitors usage, and flags churn risk before customers leave.

○ Rolling out
